# Submitting an agent to the org catalog in Microsoft 365 Copilot

## Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to **https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/agents/new**.
1. In Build your own specialist agent, click **My agents**.
1. In the list of agents, click **\<agent name\>**.
1. In Agent Builder, in the upper-right corner, click the *More options* icon (the three dots), and click **Submit to org catalog**.
1. In the message box Submit your agent to your org catalog, click **Continue**.
1. In Submit for admin approval, keep the proposed values, and click **Continue**.

    *Note:* The proposed values for **Creator website**, **Privacy statement**, and **Terms of use** are examples. A warning asks you to replace them. In a production tenant, replace them with the real addresses of your organization.

1. In Submitted for admin approval, click **Done**.

    Verify that the message shows the state Waiting for approval.

*Note:* An administrator approves or rejects the submission in the Microsoft 365 admin center. Without a submission, the agent stays private.
