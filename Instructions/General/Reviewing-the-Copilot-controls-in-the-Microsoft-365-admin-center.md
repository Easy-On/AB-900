# Reviewing the Copilot controls in the Microsoft 365 admin center

## Microsoft 365 admin center

1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to **https://admin.microsoft.com**.
1. In the Microsoft 365 admin center, in the navigation pane, expand **Copilot**, and click **Overview**.

    *Note:* If **Copilot** is not visible, click **Show all** in the navigation pane.

    Verify that the page Copilot controls shows the tabs **Overview**, **Security**, **Usage**, **Health**, and **About**.

1. On the tab Overview, note the information in the cards under **Copilot analytics**, for example **Active Copilot users**.

    *Note:* Cards can show We couldn't load this data, or Copilot isn't being used yet, as long as no usage data exists. Usage data can take up to 72 hours to appear.

1. Click the tab **Security**.
1. On the tab Security, under **Prevent data leaks**, review the **Protection summary**, and note the names of the policies that are running.

    Verify that the protection summary lists protections, for example **Protect sensitive Microsoft 365 Copilot and Chat interactions**.

1. Click the tab **Health**.
1. On the tab Health, review the cards **Update channels**, **Connected experiences status**, and **OneDrive status**.

    *Note:* The cards show Data is not available as long as the organization is new to Copilot. Monthly data is available after the first month of usage.
