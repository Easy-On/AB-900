# Deleting a chat in Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

## Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to **https://copilot.cloud.microsoft**.
1. In Microsoft 365 Copilot, under **Chats** in the navigation pane, point to **\<chat name\>**, and click the *More* icon (the three dots icon).
1. Click **Delete**.
1. In the message box Delete chat?, click **Delete**.

    *Note:* The chat is deleted permanently. Files and emails that the chat referred to are not deleted.

    Verify that **\<chat name\>** is no longer listed under **Chats**.
