# Starting a chat with a predefined agent in Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

## Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to **https://copilot.cloud.microsoft**.
1. In Microsoft 365 Copilot, click **New chat**.
1. Click the *Plus* icon (the **+** icon) to the left of the field **Message Copilot**, and click **Chat with agents and skills**.
1. In the dialog Add content, on the tab **Agents & Skills**, click **\<agent name\>**, for example **Microsoft 365 Admin**.

    Verify that the agent **\<agent name\>** is shown in the field below the text, and that the field is named **Message \<agent name\>**.

1. Click the field **Message \<agent name\>**, wait two seconds, and type **\<prompt\>**.
1. Press Enter, and wait until the answer is complete. This can take up to 30 seconds.

    Verify that the answer is shown under the name of the agent **\<agent name\>**.

*Note:* The list on the tab **Agents & Skills** contains the predefined agents and the agents that you or your organization created, for example **Excel (Agent)** and **PowerPoint (Agent)**.
