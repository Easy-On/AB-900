# Drafting an email with Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

## Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to **https://copilot.cloud.microsoft**.
1. In Microsoft 365 Copilot, click **New chat**.
1. Click the field **Message Copilot**, wait two seconds, and type **\<prompt\>**, for example **Write a short, friendly email to the sales team that announces the new document Contoso Marketing Plan.**
1. Press Enter, and wait until the answer is complete.

    Verify that Copilot shows one or more email drafts in an Outlook card with the fields **To** and the buttons **Discard** and **Send**.

    *Important:* Do not click **Send**. Copilot Chat does not send the email until you click **Send**.

1. Click the field **Message Copilot**, type **\<follow-up prompt\>**, for example **Make the email more formal and shorter.**, and press Enter.

    Verify that Copilot shows a new draft that matches the follow-up prompt.
