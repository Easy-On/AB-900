# Changing the status of a recommended action in Microsoft Secure Score

## Microsoft Defender portal

1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to **https://security.microsoft.com**.
1. In the Microsoft Defender portal, in the navigation pane, find and click **Secure score**. If you do not see **Secure score**, expand **Exposure management**, and click **Secure score**.
1. Click the tab **Recommended actions**.
1. On the tab Recommended actions, click the name **\<recommended action\>** to open its details.
1. In the details pane of the recommended action, click **Edit status & action plan**.
1. In Status & action plan, under **Status**, select **\<status\>**, for example **Planned** or **Risk accepted**.
1. Under **Action plan**, type **\<notes\>**, and click **Save and close**.

    Verify that the details pane shows the status **\<status\>** below the name of the action and the notes under **Action plan**.

1. In Recommended actions, close the details pane by clicking the **X** icon.
1. Reload the page by pressing F5.

    Verify that the column **Status** shows **\<status\>** for **\<recommended action\>**.

    *Note:* The list can show the old status until you reload the page.
