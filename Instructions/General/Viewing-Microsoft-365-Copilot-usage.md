# Viewing Microsoft 365 Copilot usage

## Microsoft 365 admin center

1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to **https://admin.microsoft.com**.
1. In the navigation pane, expand **Reports**, and click **Usage**. If you do not see **Reports**, click **Show all** first.
1. In Usage, under **Reports**, expand **Microsoft 365 Copilot**, and click **Copilot**.
1. In the Copilot report, check that the tab **Usage** is selected. If it is not selected, click the tab **Usage**.
1. On the tab Usage, under **Microsoft 365 Copilot usage**, note the numbers of **Enabled users** and **Active users**.

    Verify that the report shows the numbers of enabled users and active users.

*Note:* Usage data for a day typically becomes available within 48 hours after the end of that day (UTC). Until then, the numbers are 0, and the report can show the message Your organization doesn't have any assigned Copilot licenses, even if you assigned licenses.
