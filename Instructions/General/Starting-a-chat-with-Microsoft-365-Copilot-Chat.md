# Starting a chat with Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

## Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to **https://copilot.cloud.microsoft**.
1. In Copilot, if the page Home shows a choice of experiences, click **Chat**.

    Verify that the enterprise data protection shield is shown. The shield confirms that you use your work account.

1. In the prompt box, type **\<prompt\>**, and press **Enter**.

    Verify that Copilot shows a response to the prompt.

1. In the navigation pane, click **New chat** to start a new conversation.

    Verify that the new chat shows an empty prompt box, and that the earlier conversation is listed under **Chats** in the navigation pane.

*Note:* Copilot Chat answers only with the data that your account is allowed to access. A response can take a few seconds.
