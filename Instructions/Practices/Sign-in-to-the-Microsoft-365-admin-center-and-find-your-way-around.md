# Practice: Sign in to the Microsoft 365 admin center and find your way around

## Setup

Use a global administrator account of your tenant.

## Task

Sign in to the Microsoft 365 admin center as **admin@\<your tenant\>.onmicrosoft.com** and explore its structure:

* Identify the main areas of the navigation pane: **Home**, **Users**, **Teams & groups**, **Billing**, **Settings**, **Reports**, and **Health**.
* Open **Active users** and **Active teams & groups**, and note how many users and groups your tenant has.
* Open **Licenses** and note which subscriptions your tenant has.
* Find the page **Org settings** and note the tabs **Services** and **Security & privacy**.
* Use the search box at the top to find the page **Active users** and the page **Domains**.
* Find the list of other admin centers, and note which admin centers are available.

Do not change any setting.

## Instructions

Perform this task in the **Microsoft 365 admin center**, in **Microsoft Edge**, on **LON-CL1**.

1. Open **Microsoft Edge**.
1. In Microsoft Edge, navigate to **https://admin.microsoft.com**.
1. Sign in as **admin@\<your tenant\>.onmicrosoft.com**.

    After signing in, you are in the **Microsoft 365 admin center**, on the home page.

1. In the Microsoft 365 admin center, note the navigation pane on the left and the cards on the home page.

    *Note:* If the navigation pane shows only some of the entries, click **Show all** to display all entries.

1. In the navigation pane, expand **Users**, and click **Active users**.

    Verify that the list shows the users of your tenant.

1. In Active users, note the number of users and the columns of the list.
1. In the navigation pane, expand **Teams & groups**, and click **Active teams & groups**.

    Verify that the list shows the Microsoft 365 groups and teams of your tenant. The tabs **Distribution list** and **Security groups** show the other group types.

1. In the navigation pane, expand **Billing**, and click **Licenses**.

    Verify that the list shows the subscriptions of your tenant with the numbers of available and assigned licenses.

1. In the navigation pane, expand **Settings**, and click **Org settings**.
1. In Org settings, on the tab Services, note the list of services.
1. Click the tab **Security & privacy**.
1. On the tab Security & privacy, note the list of settings.
1. In the Microsoft 365 admin center, click the search box at the top, type **Domains**, and click the result **Domains**.

    Verify that the page Domains shows the domain **\<your tenant\>.onmicrosoft.com**.

1. Click the search box at the top, type **Active users**, and click the result **Active users**.

    Verify that the list of users opens again.

1. In the navigation pane, scroll down to the section **Admin centers**, and note the list of admin centers, for example **Security**, **Microsoft Purview**, **Identity**, and **Exchange**.

    *Note:* Each admin center opens in its own browser tab. Do not click an entry in this practice.

1. In the navigation pane, click **Home**.
1. In the Microsoft 365 admin center, click your account picture in the upper-right corner, and click **Sign out**.
