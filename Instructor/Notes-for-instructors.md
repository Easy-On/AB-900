# Notes for instructors

* The practices and labs in this repository are intended for the online lab of the course PL-7008: Create agent in Microsoft Copilot Studio.
* Practices are demonstrated by you, and the participants follow along. Labs are performed by the participants on their own.
* Before the course starts, make sure that the lab environment provides the objects and licenses named in the Setup section of each practice and lab, for example a Microsoft 365 Copilot license or a SharePoint site.
